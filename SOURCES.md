## Maxest build (v2.0)

The maxest build uses the same kernel commit (`3637f4904cf5`), Kleaf revision
and toolchain (clang-r510928, LLD 18, pahole v1.25) as the stock build above. Its maxest-specific source delta is the 21-patch series
in `patches/maxest/`; its configuration is `configs/maxest.config` and the
fragment recipe in `configs/maxest.fragments/`. SukiSU Ultra, SUSFS and KPM
follow the existing integration recipe; their sources are not redistributed
here, and the patch series is not a standalone fork tree.
